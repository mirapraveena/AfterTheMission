What the status labels mean

Left in place
Deliberately left behind as part of the plan, like Eagle's descent stage, which served as a launch pad.
Inactive on the surface
Landed and worked, then lost power or contact. Still sitting where it stopped.
Grounded, out of contact
Can no longer move or fly, and no longer talks to Earth. Ingenuity was programmed to keep recording data on board.
Silent, still travelling
No longer sending signals, but still moving through space.
