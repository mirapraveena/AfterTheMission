AftertheMission

The stories of NASA's forgotten explorers.

When a mission ends, the machine doesn't disappear. Landers still stand where they touched down. Rovers sit parked beside their last tracks. One spacecraft is still flying away from the Sun. This is where they are, and what they did.

Where they are now

    Three places they remain

    Each destination shapes what happens to a machine after its mission. On the airless Moon, nothing wears it away. On Mars, dust slowly settles on it. In deep space, nothing stops it.
