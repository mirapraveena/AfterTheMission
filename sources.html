Sources and credits

Every fact in a "Mission record" panel comes from the references below, mostly official NASA and JPL pages. The "Imagined voice" quotes are creative writing by the project team and are not historical records.

How we checked the facts

  • Dates, numbers and current status were checked against NASA mission pages, NASA news releases and the NASA Space Science Data Coordinated Archive (NSSDCA).
  • Launch dates, landing dates and end-of-mission dates are kept separate. Where the US date and the UTC date differ, the story says which one it uses.
  • Where something can't be confirmed, such as whether Ingenuity is still recording data today, the story says so instead of guessing.
  • Narration was written to match the record. It never adds events, conversations or discoveries.
